Typical 2026 pricing for garage door repair in Northwest Tampa runs from about $150 for a simple sensor or remote fix to $450 or more for a spring replacement on a double door. Most single-spring jobs land in the $250 to $350 range, and a full opener replacement with installation usually runs $400 to $800 depending on the model. Cable replacement is often $200 to $350 for both sides. What moves the number is door size, spring type, and whether we are replacing one part or a worn set. We quote before we start, and the price you hear is the price you pay.

Phase 01

Door size and weight

A 16-foot double door uses heavier springs and more hardware than a single 8 or 9-foot door, so parts cost more. Heavier insulated doors also need higher-cycle springs, which adds to the price but lasts longer. We size the spring to the actual door weight, not a guess.

Phase 02

Spring type and cycle rating

Standard torsion springs cost less than high-cycle springs rated for 20,000 or more cycles. In Northwest Tampa, where doors cycle several times a day, upgrading to a higher-cycle spring often pays for itself. We explain the difference and let you choose.

Phase 03

Time of day and urgency

A scheduled weekday repair costs less than an after-hours emergency call. If your door is stuck open and the house is not secure, the emergency rate is worth it. If it can wait until morning, we will tell you and save you the difference.
