What's included

What is included in a garage door tune up in Tampa

Every tune-up covers the full door system, not just the opener. We check the springs, cables, rollers, hinges, tracks, sensors, and opener. If we find a worn part, we quote the repair before we touch it. Here is what you get.

01

Full lubrication service

We apply a garage door lubricant to the torsion springs, hinges, rollers, and opener gears. We also clean the tracks so grit does not grind the rollers. This is the step that quiets most noisy doors.

02

Hardware tightening

Using a garage door torque wrench, we tighten every bolt on the hinges, brackets, and cable drums. Loose hardware is the leading cause of doors that shudder or drift off track.

03

Spring and cable check

We measure torsion spring tension, inspect the winding cones, and look for frayed galvanized steel cables. If a spring is within 20 percent of failing, we tell you before it snaps.

04

Safety sensor testing

We test the photo eye sensors with a garage door sensor alignment tool and verify the auto-reverse works. We also check the wall mount keypad and the opener remote tester signal.

05

Written condition report

You get a written report listing every part we checked, the spring tension reading, and any part that will need replacement in the next 6 to 12 months. No guesswork.

Our 5-step garage door tune up process

The process flexes to the condition, but these checkpoints keep decisions clear.

01

Visual inspection

We walk the door, check the panels for dents, look at the garage door tracks for bends, and inspect the bottom rubber seal. We also test the garage door opener remote tester to confirm the signal reaches the motor. This takes about 10 minutes.

Stage 1
02

Lubrication and cleaning

We spray a lithium-based garage door lubricant on the torsion springs, hinges, and rollers. We wipe down the tracks and remove grit that causes grinding. This step alone quiets most noisy doors and takes 15 to 20 minutes.

Stage 2
03

Hardware adjustment

Using a garage door torque wrench, we tighten every bolt on the hinges and the cable drums. We adjust the track bender where the door rubs and set the torsion spring tension with garage door winding bars. This is the step that prevents off-track doors.

Stage 3
04

Safety testing

We test the photo eye sensors with a garage door sensor alignment tool, check the auto-reverse, and verify the wall mount keypad works. If the door fails the UL 325 safety standard, we fix it before we leave. This takes 10 to 15 minutes.

Stage 4

Final cycle and report

We run the door up and down three times, listen for any remaining noise, and hand you a written report. The report lists every part we checked, the spring tension reading, and any part that will need replacement in the next 6 to 12 months.

Tools & methods

Tools and methods we use on every tune-up

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Garage door winding bars

Used to safely adjust torsion spring tension. We never adjust springs without them because a slipped spring can cause serious injury.

02

Garage door torque wrench

We torque every hinge bolt and bracket to the manufacturer's spec. This prevents the vibration that leads to loose hardware and off-track doors.

03

Garage door sensor alignment tool

Aligns the photo eye sensors so the door closes reliably. A misaligned sensor is the most common reason a door reverses halfway down.

04

Garage door track bender

Straightens bent tracks without removing them. This saves time and keeps the door running smooth after a minor impact.

05

Garage door opener remote tester

Confirms the remote signal reaches the opener. We use it to diagnose remotes that only work from a few feet away.

06

Garage door roller removal tool

Lets us swap worn rollers quickly without bending the track. We replace steel rollers with nylon garage door rollers for quieter operation.

Problems we solve

Problems we solve with a Tampa garage door tune up

Most garage door failures start as small symptoms. A tune-up catches them before they become emergencies. Here are the issues we see most often in Tampa.

ISSUE

Door shudders or jerks when opening

We lubricate the rollers, tighten the hinges, and adjust the track alignment. If the rollers are worn, we replace them with nylon garage door rollers on the same visit.

SYMPTOM

Loud grinding or squealing noise

We apply a lithium-based garage door lubricant to the springs, hinges, and rollers. We also check the cable drums for slack and reset the tension if needed.

SIGN

Door reverses for no reason

We realign the sensors with a garage door sensor alignment tool, clean the lenses, and test the auto-reverse against UL 325 safety standard. If the sensor is bad, we replace it.

ALERT

Opener runs but door does not move

We diagnose the opener gears and the spring system. If the spring is broken, we replace it with proper winding bars and rebalance the door. If the opener is failing, we repair or replace it.
