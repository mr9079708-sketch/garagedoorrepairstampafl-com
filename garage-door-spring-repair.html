What Our Spring Repair Service Includes

Every spring job is different, but here is what is standard when we replace a torsion or extension spring in Tampa. If we find something extra, like a frayed cable or a cracked hinge, we show you and quote it before we do the work.

01

Matched Spring Pair

We replace both torsion springs as a pair, even if only one broke. The second spring has the same cycle count and will fail soon. Matching them keeps the door balanced and prevents a repeat breakdown.

02

Correct Winding and Tension

We wind the new springs to the manufacturer's specified number of turns using garage door torsion spring winding bars. Too few turns and the door is heavy; too many and it flies up. We test the balance at the halfway point.

03

Cable and Drum Inspection

We check the galvanized steel cables for fraying and make sure they are seated on the cable drums. If a cable is worn, we will quote a replacement. A bad cable can drop a door just like a broken spring.

04

Safety Reverse Test

After the springs are on, we test the photo eye sensors and the auto-reverse with a piece of wood on the floor. If the door does not reverse, we adjust the opener before we leave. This is a UL 325 requirement and a basic safety check.

05

Lubrication and Cleanup

We lubricate the springs, hinges, and rollers with garage door lubricant, wipe down the tracks, and haul away the old springs. Your garage floor is left clean, and the door runs quieter than before.

What Garage Door Spring Repair in Tampa Really Involves

Garage door spring repair in Tampa is the replacement or resetting of the torsion or extension springs that counterbalance your door's weight. A standard 16x7 door carries two torsion springs, each rated for roughly 10,000 to 20,000 cycles. When one breaks, the door may still open a few feet before jamming, or it may not move at all. We measure the old spring's wire gauge, inside diameter, and length, then match it exactly so the door balances at the midpoint of travel.

In Tampa, heat and humidity age springs faster than most homeowners expect. A door that sits in a west-facing garage bakes at 120 degrees or more through the summer, and that thermal cycling fatigues the steel. Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use on Spring Jobs

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Garage Door Torsion Spring Winding Bars

Steel bars used to safely wind and release tension on torsion springs. We carry the correct diameter for each spring size so the bars fit snug and do not slip.

02

Garage Door Cable Drums and Winding Cones

We replace worn drums and cones when the cable has jumped or the drum is cracked. These parts control how the cable wraps and are critical to a safe lift.

03

Garage Door Torque Wrench

Used to set the correct tension on spring bolts and track hardware. Over-tightening can strip bolts; under-tightening lets parts rattle loose.

04

Garage Door Roller Removal Tool

Lets us swap rollers without bending the track. We replace worn nylon or steel rollers during a spring job when they are noisy or flat-spotted.

05

Garage Door Sensor Alignment Tool

Aligns the photo eye sensors so the door closes reliably. We use it after every spring replacement to make sure the safety beam is true.

06

Garage Door Track Bender

Straightens minor track dents and adjusts the track radius. A bent track can stop a door even with new springs, so we fix it on the spot when we can.

Problems we solve

Spring Problems We Fix Every Week in Tampa

Springs fail in predictable ways, and each failure has a specific fix. Here are the most common spring problems we see on Tampa doors.

ISSUE

Door opens a foot then stops

We replace the broken spring and its partner, wind them to the correct tension, and test the door balance. We also check the opener for strain damage and reset the travel limits if needed.

SYMPTOM

Loud bang from the garage

We come out, clamp the door, and replace both springs. We inspect the cables and drums for damage from the sudden release, then run a full safety test before we leave.

SIGN

Door feels extremely heavy by hand

We measure the spring tension and cycle count. If the springs are near the end of their life, we replace them with high-cycle springs rated for 20,000 cycles or more, which is a good upgrade for Tampa heat.

ALERT

Door is crooked or binds on one side

We reset the cables on the drums, replace any damaged rollers, and rebalance the springs so both sides lift evenly. We also check the track alignment and adjust it if needed.

Pricing process

What Spring Repair Costs in Tampa

$120 - $450 for most residential spring replacements

Phase 01

Spring Type and Cycle Rating

Standard 10,000-cycle springs are the least expensive. High-cycle springs rated for 20,000 to 30,000 cycles cost more up front but last two to three times as long. For a busy Tampa household, the upgrade often pays for itself.

Phase 02

Door Size and Weight

A single-car door uses smaller, cheaper springs than an 8-foot double door. Heavier wood doors or doors with insulation need higher-tension springs, which cost more. We measure the door and quote the correct spring for your setup.

Phase 03

Number of Springs

Most modern doors have two torsion springs, and we replace them as a pair. Some older or lighter doors have a single spring. The price reflects the number of springs and the labor to wind them correctly.
