Phase 01

Spring type and cycle rating

Standard torsion springs cost less than high-cycle or custom-wound springs. On the older doors common in Seminole Heights, we often recommend a higher-cycle spring because it lasts longer and costs only a little more up front.

Phase 02

Door weight and material

A solid wood door from the 1920s weighs far more than a modern steel door, which means heavier springs, more labor and sometimes a two-person job. Insulated and glass-panel doors also add weight and cost.

Phase 03

Access and job complexity

A door in a tight alley or a garage packed with stored items takes longer to work around. Off-track doors, bent tracks and stripped hinges add labor. We factor access in when we quote so there are no surprises.
