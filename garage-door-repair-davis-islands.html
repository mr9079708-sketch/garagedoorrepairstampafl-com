Garage Door Spring Repair

Torsion springs on island doors fail faster because of salt air and constant heat cycling. We replace both springs, not just the broken one, and we use garage door torsion spring winding bars and a torque wrench to set the correct tension. Most spring jobs are done in under two hours.

Garage Door Opener Repair

LiftMaster and Chamberlain openers are the most common on Davis Islands, and most failures trace back to worn gears or a logic board that took on moisture. We test the opener under load, not just at the wall button, and we replace garage door opener gears on site.

Garage Door Cable Repair

A snapped garage door cable usually means the door is hanging by one side. We replace galvanized steel cables in pairs, reset the cable drums, and check the winding cones while the door is apart. Same-day cable repair is standard for us on the island.

05

Garage Door Panel Repair

Dented garage door panels are common on narrow island driveways where a bumper catches the bottom section. If the panel is a current Clopay or Amarr profile, we can often swap just that section. If it is discontinued, we match the closest panel and blend the finish.

06

Garage Door Roller Repair

Noisy garage door rollers are the first sign of trouble on coastal doors. Steel rollers rust and flat-spot; nylon rollers wear unevenly and drop the door out of alignment. We swap rollers with a garage door roller removal tool and rebalance the door before we leave.

Narrow driveways and low-clearance garages

Phase 01

Door size and weight

A double-car door needs heavier springs and a stronger opener than a single door. On Davis Islands, where many homes have oversized two-car garages, the parts cost more and the labor time is longer. We size the springs to the actual door weight, not a generic chart.

Phase 02

Part availability and corrosion grade

Coastal-rated springs, nylon rollers, and galvanized cables cost more than standard parts, but they last longer in salt air. We stock the coastal-grade parts on the truck so you are not waiting on an order. If a discontinued panel has to be sourced, that adds time and cost.

Phase 03

Access and clearance

Narrow island driveways and low-clearance garages can add labor time. If we have to work around a parked car or use a low-headroom track setup, the job takes longer. We check access when we quote so there are no surprises.
