What moves the number is the door itself: a heavy wooden door on an older detached garage needs bigger springs and more labor than a modern steel door. Phase 01

Door weight and size

A single-car door takes smaller, cheaper springs than a 16x7 two-car door. Older solid wood doors can weigh twice what a modern steel door does, which means heavier springs, heavier cables and more labor. We weigh the door before we quote.

Phase 02

Spring type and quantity

Torsion springs cost more than extension springs, and we always replace both at once. A single torsion spring replacement on a standard door runs $250 to $400 installed. If the door has two springs, doing both now saves you a second service call later.

Phase 03

After-hours and emergency calls

Emergency garage door repair in Plant City carries an after-hours rate, usually $75 to $150 on top of the repair. We quote that before we roll, so you can decide whether to wait until morning. If the door will not secure, we treat it as urgent.
