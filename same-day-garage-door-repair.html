Problems we solve

Common Garage Door Problems We Solve

From broken springs to faulty sensors, we've seen every garage door issue. Here are some of the most common problems we fix for Tampa homeowners.

ISSUE

Broken torsion spring

We replace the broken spring with a high-cycle replacement. We use proper winding bars and safety techniques. Most spring replacements take 1-2 hours and come with a warranty.

SYMPTOM

Snapped garage door cable

We replace the galvanized steel cable and re-spool it on the drum. We also inspect the other cable and replace if worn. This restores smooth operation.

SIGN

Garage door off track

We safely get the door back on track. We inspect rollers, hinges, and tracks for damage. If parts are bent, we replace them. This is often an emergency repair.

ALERT

Garage door opener not working

We diagnose the opener, checking the motor, gears, logic board, and safety sensors. We repair or replace the unit. We stock parts for LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, and Craftsman.
