01

High humidity rusts steel rollers and tracks

Brandon's humid air causes steel rollers to seize and tracks to rust, which makes the door grind and bind. We see this most on doors older than eight years that have never been lubricated. It shortens the life of the hardware and puts extra strain on the opener. How we help: We swap rusted steel rollers for nylon garage door rollers that resist moisture, clean and lubricate the tracks, and apply a garage door lubricant rated for Florida heat. If the track is too far gone, we replace the section and realign the door.

02

Summer storms cause power surges that fry openers

Afternoon thunderstorms are a weekly event in Brandon from June through September. Power surges travel through the wall outlet and damage the opener's logic board or receiver. Homeowners often find their remote no longer works even though the wall button does. How we help: We test the opener with a remote tester and inspect the logic board for surge damage. If the board is fried, we replace it or install a new opener with surge protection. We also recommend a whole-home surge protector to prevent repeat failures.

03

HOA rules restrict panel styles and colors

Many Brandon subdivisions have HOA regulations that dictate garage door color, panel style, and even the type of opener. Installing the wrong panel can mean a fine and a redo. This adds time to the job if we have to order a specific panel. How we help: We ask about HOA rules before we order parts. We match Clopay, Amarr, and Wayne Dalton panels to the approved style and color. If you are not sure what your HOA allows, we can point you to the right contact so you get approval before we install.

Type of spring

Torsion springs cost more than extension springs because they require more labor and specialized tools like winding bars. High-cycle springs cost a bit more but last longer in Brandon's heat. We always recommend replacing both springs at once.

Phase 02

Door size and weight

A single-car door is cheaper to repair than a two-car door. Heavier doors need stronger springs and sometimes upgraded hardware. If your door is insulated or made of wood, it will weigh more and may need a higher-cycle spring.

Phase 03

Emergency calls on nights, weekends, or holidays carry a higher rate. If your door is stuck open or off track, it is usually worth it for security. We will give you the after-hours rate before we dispatch a tech.
