Garage Door Repair Costs in North Tampa

Typical 2026 pricing for garage door repair in North Tampa runs from about $150 for a simple roller or sensor fix up to $600 or more for a spring replacement with new cables. Most single-visit jobs land in the $180 to $450 range. What pushes the number up is door weight, how many parts failed together, and whether the opener needs work too. A two-car door with heavy insulated panels costs more to re-spring than a single-car wood door. We quote the job before we start, and the price does not change once we are on site unless you approve extra work. If you want affordable garage door repair in North Tampa without surprises, that is how we run it.

Phase 01

Door size and weight

A two-car insulated door needs heavier springs and a stronger opener than a single-car door. That raises the parts cost, and it takes longer to balance. We size the springs to the actual door weight, not a generic chart.

Phase 02

Number of failed parts

When a spring breaks, it often takes a cable with it. Replacing both at once is cheaper than two separate visits, and it is the right call anyway. We tell you what failed and what is about to fail so you can decide.

Phase 03

Opener condition

If the opener has been fighting an unbalanced door, the gears and chain may be worn. Replacing the opener adds to the bill, but ignoring it means the new springs will wear faster. We check it before we quote.
