Garage Door Repair Cost in Valrico

Typical 2026 pricing for garage door repair in Valrico runs $180 to $450 for most common jobs. A torsion spring replacement on a standard two-car door is usually $250 to $400 depending on wire gauge and whether we replace one spring or both. Cable replacement runs $150 to $300. Phase 01

Door weight and size

A heavier insulated door needs thicker springs and heavier hardware, which costs more than a basic steel door. In Valrico, most two-car garages fall in the middle, but Lithia homes with oversized doors sit at the top of the range.

Phase 02

Single vs. matched spring replacement

We always recommend replacing torsion springs in pairs. If one snapped, the other has the same number of cycles on it and is likely close behind. Doing both at once costs a bit more today but saves a second service call within a year.

Phase 03

Damage beyond the failed part

A snapped cable can bend a track or flatten rollers. If the door came down crooked, there is usually more to fix than the cable itself. We inspect the whole system and quote the full job so there are no add-ons later.
