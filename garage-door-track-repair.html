The basics

What Is Garage Door Track Repair?

The metal rails that guide the door up and down are what track repair is about. A rail bends when the door is hit, drifts out of alignment as the house settles, or wears where rollers have run in the same spot for years. Sticking, scraping, running crooked, or coming off the track entirely are the signs. The fix is straightening or replacing the bent section, realigning the tracks level and plumb, and checking the rollers so the door travels without binding.

What's included

What Our Track Repair Service Includes

Alignment work, a hardware check, and a safety test come with every track repair. A worn roller, cable, or spring found along the way is shown to you and priced before it is touched.

01

Track Plumb and Level Check

Both vertical rails are checked for plumb and the horizontal rails for level, and the gap is measured at every roller pocket. Anything out by more than an eighth of an inch is corrected. That measurement is what decides whether the door runs smooth or grinds.

02

Straightening or Rail Replacement

Small bends are corrected with a track bender and fresh lag bolts into solid framing. Kinked or rusted rails are replaced with new 2-inch galvanized track cut to the door height. A kink that would fold again is never bent back.

03

Roller and Hardware Inspection

Every roller is checked for flat spots, cracks, and bearing play, and the bad ones are replaced with nylon or steel rollers to match the door. Hinge bolts are torqued, cable drums checked, and the bottom rubber seal looked at while we are down there.

04

Opener Travel Reset

Moving the track usually throws the opener limits off. Up and down travel is reset on LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, and Craftsman units so the door stops flush and opens fully. That is included, not an add-on.

05

Safety System Test

Photo eye sensors, the auto-reverse, and the door balance are tested by hand at the halfway point. Everything is checked against UL 325 before we leave. A misaligned sensor or frayed cable is shown to you and quoted.

Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use

Track work is only as good as the gear behind it. These are the tools that let the job be done right the first time.

01

Garage door track bender

Corrects small bends in the vertical and horizontal rail without kinking the steel. Even pressure along the bend keeps the track true.

02

Garage door winding bars

Safely releases and resets torsion spring tension when the track work needs the door down. A wound spring is never worked on without them.

03

Garage door roller removal tool

Rollers are pulled and seated without bending the track tab. Swaps go faster, and the roller pocket stays square.

04

Garage door torque wrench

Hinge and lag bolts are brought to spec with this. A lag bolt driven too hard splits the jamb, and one left loose lets the track walk out of plumb.

05

Garage door sensor alignment tool

Photo eye sensors are aligned after track work moves the door. A sensor knocked out of line stops the door mid-cycle.

06

Garage door opener remote tester

Confirms the remote and receiver are talking once the opener travel is reset. It saves a callback when the remote was the real problem all along.

Problems we solve

Track Problems We Fix Every Week in Tampa

Track calls come down to a handful of failures. Here is what turns up, what happens when it is ignored, and how it is fixed.

ISSUE

Door is off track and hanging crooked

The door goes back on the rail, the bent section is replaced, and the cable and spring on that side are checked. The opener should not be cycled until a tech has seen it. This is the most common emergency call in Tampa.

SYMPTOM

Door jerks and grinds on one side

The binding roller is found, replaced, and the vertical track on that side realigned. Most of these jobs finish in under two hours, and a straining opener gets its travel limits reset too.

SIGN

Track is rusted and flaking near the floor

The lower track section is replaced with new galvanized steel and the cut edge sealed. When both verticals are rusted, the full set goes in. Older doors in Davis Islands and Ballast Point see this often.

ALERT

Door is stuck halfway open

The opener is released, the track and rollers checked, and the binding point found, usually a bent rail or a seized roller. It is fixed, the full cycle tested, and the opener reset before we leave.

Why is my garage door track bent in Tampa?

The two most common causes are a vehicle backing into the rail and a roller that seized and bent the track from the inside. Salt air near the bay also rusts the rail thinner, so it kinks easier. If your door is off track and leaning, stop running the opener and call us. We see this weekly in South Tampa and Davis Islands driveways.

How much does garage door track repair cost in Tampa?

Typical 2026 pricing in this area runs $150 to $450 for realignment and $300 to $700 when a rail has to be replaced. Two-car doors with long horizontal track sit at the higher end. The price depends on how many rails are damaged, whether rollers need swapping, and if the door is a wind-rated model. You get the number before we start.

Can I fix a bent garage door track myself in Tampa?

You can tighten loose lag bolts and clear debris from the track yourself. Straightening a kinked rail is a different job. Track steel is under load from the door, and a bad bend can drop the door or snap a cable. We carry a garage door track bender and winding bars for a reason. Call a licensed tech for anything past a loose bolt.

Who repairs garage door tracks in Tampa?

We do, across Hillsborough County and the surrounding areas. Our techs handle realignment, full rail replacement, roller swaps, and the opener reset that usually follows a track problem. We are a garage door contractor (licence number available on request). Call us and we will tell you on the phone whether it is a repair or a replacement.

What causes a garage door track to bend in Tampa?

The most common cause is a car bumping the door or the track itself. A broken roller or cable can also push the door sideways and bend the track, and a door that has run out of alignment for a long time wears the track unevenly. In some cases the track was never fitted quite straight, and the bend appears over time as the door keeps running through the same spot.

Can a bent garage door track be straightened, or does it need replacing in Tampa?

A slight bend can often be straightened on site, as long as the metal is not creased or cracked. A track that is badly bent, creased, or rusted through should be replaced, since a straightened crease is a weak point that can bend again. The technician checks the track along its full length and tells you honestly whether a repair will last or whether a new section is the better choice.

How do I keep my garage door tracks in good shape in Tampa?

Keep the tracks clean and free of leaves, dirt, and debris, and never lubricate the inside of the track, since grease collects grit and makes the rollers stick. Check the bolts that hold the track to the wall now and then, and tighten any that have worked loose. If the door starts to scrape or bind, have it checked early, since a small alignment problem is a quick fix and a bent track is not.
