The basics

What Is Garage Door Track Repair?

Garage door track repair fixes the metal rails that guide the door up and down. Tracks bend when the door is hit, shift out of alignment as the house settles, or wear where rollers have run in the same spot for years. You need it when the door sticks, scrapes, runs crooked, or comes off the track entirely. Repair means straightening or replacing the bent section, realigning the tracks so they are level and plumb, and checking the rollers so the door travels smoothly without binding.

What's included

What Our Track Repair Service Includes

Every track repair includes the alignment work, the hardware check, and the safety test. If we find a worn roller, cable, or spring while we are in there, we will show you and price it before we touch it.

01

Track Plumb and Level Check

We check both vertical rails for plumb and the horizontal rails for level, then measure the gap at every roller pocket. Anything off by more than an eighth of an inch gets corrected. This is the measurement that decides whether your door runs smooth or grinds.

02

Straightening or Rail Replacement

Minor bends get corrected with a garage door track bender and fresh lag bolts into solid framing. Kinked or rusted rails get replaced with new 2-inch galvanized track cut to your door height. We do not bend back a kink that will fold again.

03

Roller and Hardware Inspection

We check every roller for flat spots, cracks, and bearing play, and replace the bad ones with nylon or steel rollers to match your door. Hinge bolts get torqued, cable drums get checked, and the bottom rubber seal gets a look while we are down there.

04

Opener Travel Reset

After the track moves, the opener limits are usually off. We reset the up and down travel on LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, and Craftsman units so the door stops flush and opens fully. This is included, not an add-on.

05

Safety System Test

We test the photo eye sensors, the auto-reverse, and the door balance by hand at the halfway point. Everything gets checked against UL 325 before we leave. If a sensor is misaligned or a cable is frayed, we will show you and quote the fix.

Tools & methods

Tools and Methods We Use

Purpose-built equipment, used by trained technicians, so the job is done accurately the first time.

01

Garage door track bender

Used to correct minor bends in the vertical and horizontal rail without kinking the steel. It applies even pressure along the bend so the track stays true.

02

Garage door winding bars

Used to safely release and reset torsion spring tension when the track work requires the door to come down. Never work on a wound spring without them.

03

Garage door roller removal tool

Lets us pull and seat rollers without bending the track tab. It speeds up roller swaps and keeps the roller pocket square.

04

Garage door torque wrench

Used to torque hinge and lag bolts to spec. Over-tightened lag bolts split the jamb, and loose ones let the track walk out of plumb.

05

Garage door sensor alignment tool

Aligns the photo eye sensors after track work moves the door. A sensor knocked out of line will stop the door mid-cycle.

06

Garage door opener remote tester

Confirms the remote and receiver are talking after we reset the opener travel. Saves you a callback if the remote was the real problem.

Number of damaged rails

Why is my garage door track bent in Tampa?

The two most common causes are a vehicle backing into the rail and a roller that seized and bent the track from the inside. Salt air near the bay also rusts the rail thinner, so it kinks easier. If your door is off track and leaning, stop running the opener and call us. We see this weekly in South Tampa and Davis Islands driveways.

How much does garage door track repair cost in Tampa?

Typical 2026 pricing in this area runs $150 to $450 for realignment and $300 to $700 when a rail has to be replaced. Two-car doors with long horizontal track sit at the higher end. The price depends on how many rails are damaged, whether rollers need swapping, and if the door is a wind-rated model. You get the number before we start.

Can I fix a bent garage door track myself in Tampa?

You can tighten loose lag bolts and clear debris from the track yourself. Straightening a kinked rail is a different job. Track steel is under load from the door, and a bad bend can drop the door or snap a cable. We carry a garage door track bender and winding bars for a reason. Call a licensed tech for anything past a loose bolt.

We do, across Hillsborough County and the surrounding areas. Our techs handle realignment, full rail replacement, roller swaps, and the opener reset that usually follows a track problem. What causes a garage door track to bend in Tampa?

The most common cause is a car bumping the door or the track itself. A broken roller or cable can also push the door sideways and bend the track, and a door that has run out of alignment for a long time wears the track unevenly. In some cases the track was never fitted quite straight, and the bend appears over time as the door keeps running through the same spot.

Can a bent garage door track be straightened, or does it need replacing in Tampa?

A slight bend can often be straightened on site, as long as the metal is not creased or cracked. A track that is badly bent, creased, or rusted through should be replaced, since a straightened crease is a weak point that can bend again. The technician checks the track along its full length and tells you honestly whether a repair will last or whether a new section is the better choice.

How do I keep my garage door tracks in good shape in Tampa?

Keep the tracks clean and free of leaves, dirt, and debris, and never lubricate the inside of the track, since grease collects grit and makes the rollers stick. Check the bolts that hold the track to the wall now and then, and tighten any that have worked loose. If the door starts to scrape or bind, have it checked early, since a small alignment problem is a quick fix and a bent track is not.
