Phase 01

Door size and weight

A wide two-car door needs heavier springs and cables than a single-car door, which raises the parts cost. Doors with insulation or glass panels weigh more too. We match the hardware to the actual weight so the repair lasts.

Phase 02

Type of spring or opener

Torsion springs cost more than extension springs but last longer, and high-cycle springs cost a bit more up front. On openers, belt drives run quieter and cost more than chain drives. We'll tell you the trade-off in plain terms.

Phase 03

Time of day and urgency

A standard weekday appointment is the base rate. After-hours and emergency calls carry a higher rate because we're pulling a tech off the schedule. If it can wait until morning, you'll save money.
