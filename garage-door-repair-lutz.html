Humidity corrodes springs and cables year-round

Lutz sits in a humid subtropical climate, so torsion springs, extension springs and galvanized steel cables rust faster than in drier markets. A spring that would last 10 years elsewhere can fail years earlier here, and cables fray at the drum. How we help: We inspect spring coils and cable strands on every call, not just the part you reported. When we replace a spring, we replace the pair and check the drums and cones at the same time, so you are not paying a second callout in a month.

02

Larger lots with detached garages and shop doors

Many Lutz properties have a detached garage or shop building with its own door and opener, sometimes on a separate driveway. That means more doors per household, more cycles, and openers that get used heavily for lawn equipment and tools. How we help: We service both doors on a property in one visit when you book it that way, and we size openers for the actual duty cycle. For shop doors we recommend a heavier-duty unit and check the track alignment, since those doors often run less often but with more load.

03

Older doors still running original extension springs

A good share of Lutz homes were built in the 1980s and 1990s, and some still run original extension springs and steel rollers. When one extension spring breaks, the door drops hard on one side and can bend the track or crack a panel. How we help: We replace extension springs in matched pairs, upgrade worn steel rollers to nylon where it helps, and check the track for the bend that follows a hard drop. If the door is out of balance, we reset it before it cycles again.

04

Storm-season power flickers and opener board damage

Summer thunderstorms roll through Hillsborough County regularly, and power surges take out opener logic boards, remotes and keypads. A door that suddenly will not respond is often an electronics problem, not a mechanical one. How we help: We test the opener board, wall mount keypad and photo eye sensors before recommending replacement. Surge-damaged boards can often be swapped without a new opener, which saves you money. We also check the safety reverse after any electrical fault.
